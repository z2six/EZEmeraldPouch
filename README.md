<p>The mod is simple, so I'll keep it short.</p>

<p>
If you've tried currency mods before (Lightman's, Numismatic, etc),
you'll usually run into the same problem:
they don't work with <em>all</em> of your mods.
Or they add a lot of extra systems, blocks, GUIs, and mechanics you don't actually want.
</p>

<p>Hence, <strong>EZ Emerald Pouch</strong>.</p>

<p>
No currencies.<br>
No economy overhaul.<br>
Just emeralds, done cleanly.
</p>

<p><strong>How it works:</strong></p>

<ol>
  <li>
    Craft an Emerald Pouch
    <br>
    <img src="https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/null/description_0ab4e1c7-af44-40b5-9ac7-bcf9af6c74ea.png"
         alt="crafting-recipe"
         width="239"
         height="113">
  </li>
</ol>

<p>
2. Store emeralds into your pouch:
</p>
<ul>
  <li>Picking up emeralds automatically stores them</li>
  <li><strong>SHIFT + Right Click</strong> an Emerald or Emerald Block in your inventory to deposit it</li>
</ul>

<p>
The pouch stores an <strong>infinite amount</strong> of emeralds.
</p>

<p>
3. While trading (or whenever your inventory is open), click the Withdraw button to take out emeralds.
<br>
<strong>SHIFT + Click</strong> withdraws multiple stacks.
</p>

<p>
  <img src="https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/null/description_9d5c91a1-8073-4aec-94e2-747b6630bafd.png"
       alt="withdraw-emeralds"
       width="163"
       height="109">
</p>

<p>
4. Press <strong>O</strong> to edit the GUI elements:
</p>
<ul>
  <li>Move the emerald counter</li>
  <li>Move the withdraw button</li>
  <li>Resize elements</li>
  <li>Hide or show elements</li>
  <li>Reset everything to defaults</li>
</ul>

<p>
All settings are <strong>client-side only</strong>.
</p>

<p>
  <img src="https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/null/description_91cda8fc-f8f9-4b58-b82d-4a5e7a76c23e.png"
       alt="settings-menu"
       width="400"
       height="242">
</p>

<p><strong>Extra notes:</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>Fully server-authoritative (no client-side emerald cheating)</li>
  <li>Optional Curios support (custom pouch slot)</li>
  <li>Designed to work alongside any mod that uses emeralds</li>
</ul>

<p>
That's it.<br>
EZ Emerald Pouch does one thing — and stays out of your way.
</p>
